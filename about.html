Bio

I'm a high school student in Georgia, and this year I'm taking AP Research. Most of what I do outside class is machine learning. I like building models from scratch so I can see what's happening inside them, and lately I've been reading a lot about how language models get tested.

I got interested in AI reliability from using chatbots on long coding projects. The first answers were usually good, but after enough back-and-forth something I had asked for earlier would quietly disappear. When I found a paper that measured the same problem across more than 200,000 conversations, I wanted to know if there's a point where a conversation can't be fixed anymore.

After this project I want to keep working on how AI systems are evaluated, especially ways to tell when a model's answer can be trusted.
